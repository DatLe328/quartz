# [kali-anonsurf](https://github.com/Und3rf10w/kali-anonsurf)
Anonsurf is an anonymization tool available on Parrot OS (also installable on Kali).
**Its goals are:**
* Force all traffic (all internet connections from your machine) to go through the Tor network.
* Hide your real IP + real DNS + real MAC.
* Help you be more anonymous when pentesting, surfing the web, hacking, etc...
